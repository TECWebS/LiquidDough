A little note from the kitchen

Welcome to my corner of the internet

My name is Tashanna, and I'm the baker behind Liquid Dough, a micro-bakery based in New London, CT. I chose the name Liquid Dough because my doughs turn quite loose once my sourdough starter joins the mix. The name kind of chose itself. I'm dedicated to crafting small-batch breads, cookies, brownies, and muffins from scratch that taste incredible.

My own journey into baking started with digestive issues that sent me looking for answers beyond what was available at the store. The more I researched, the more I realized how much of what we eat every day is processed far past the point of being recognizable to the eye.

"Once I started baking with a sourdough starter, my own stomach felt so much better — and that's how Liquid Dough began."

Today, my artisan breads are leavened with my sourdough starter in place of commercial yeast. Baking became less of a hobby and more of a way to take care of myself, one loaf at a time. Sharing these baked goods with others is so much fun, and I make sure to use the finest ingredients in everything I bake.

My specialty breads are naturally leavened with sourdough, and they sit alongside my signature homemade cookies, brownies, and muffins for a sweet balance of flavor.

— Tashanna
