Fun Facts

150

⚖️ "Flour is a shapeshifter!" When using a 1 cup scoop, your flour can weigh anywhere from 110 to 150 grams. But with a kitchen scale, a gram is always a gram. Precision = perfect cookies!

1938

🍪 The chocolate chip cookie was invented by accident in 1938! Ruth Wakefield, who ran the Toll House Inn, chopped up a chocolate bar expecting it to melt into the dough. It held its shape instead, and a classic was born.

80

🧈 Butter is at least 80% milkfat, and most of the rest is water. In the oven that water turns to steam, which gives cookies and muffins their lift and tender crumb.

5,000

🌍 Sourdough is the original bread—people have been baking with wild yeast and starter cultures for over 5,000 years!
